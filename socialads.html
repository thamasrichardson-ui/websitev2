FIFA World Cup 2026 Social Ads

Beautiful graphics for the beautiful game.

Project Details

  • Date Completed: Nov. 2025
  • Project Role: Visual Designer & Advertising Strategist
  • Software Used: Adobe Photoshop & Adobe Illustrator
  • Type of Work: Cross-Platform Digital Campaign Assets
  • Nature of Work: Assignment for Ad Design & Visual Storytelling
  • Time to Complete: 3 days

Context

This assignment required developing cross-platform social media assets for a brand of choice. I selected the FIFA World Cup 2026 to showcase visual design consistency and strategic copywriting across different formats (feed and story posts). My personal connection to the sport ensured authentic messaging.

Design

The process began with a competitive analysis of the @fifaworldcup Instagram feed to ensure brand alignment and visual consistency with their existing aesthetic. The key graphic element—the curved background shape—was created using vector paths in Illustrator for crisp scalability, and finalized with detailed image manipulation and text hierarchy in Photoshop.

For the Netherlands ad, I developed a simple pun-based copywriting strategy (Oranje You glad) centered on the team's nickname. The design then strategically used a complementary color palette to ensure the graphic supported the message and team branding.

The featured image used Lionel Messi to visually anchor the campaign. I created the multi-lingual "soccer" text in Illustrator and imported it as a Smart Object to retain vector quality. The striking visual effect of the text appearing behind the player was achieved using a layer mask and selection tool in Photoshop, demonstrating advanced image compositing skill.

Outcome

The outcome is a set of three high-impact digital campaign assets for the 2026 FIFA World Cup. These graphics demonstrate my ability to execute a unified visual campaign across different formats (feed and story). Key Strategic Insight: While developed for a US-based assignment, the final featured ad highlights the necessity of market-specific localization, requiring the copy to be adjusted from "SOCCER" to "FOOTBALL" before deployment in global markets.
